Overview

An Ethiopia that most people never see.

Most visitors come north — to the churches and castles. Southern Ethiopia is a different world: the Rift Valley Lakes teeming with flamingos and pelicans, the diverse ethnic groups of the Omo Valley, and the cloud forests of the Bale Mountains. This is the country's ecological and cultural heartland.

This is a ground-only package — you arrange your international flight into Addis, and we handle everything from the moment you land. The birding add-on is particularly popular: the Rift Valley Lakes are one of Africa's premier birding sites, and we can arrange specialist birding guides for any day of the itinerary.

Day by Day

Full Itinerary

1

Addis Ababa — Arrival & Orientation

Arrive at Bole International Airport. Your guide meets you, transfers you to your hotel, and runs a full orientation dinner covering what's ahead: the landscapes, the cultures, what to expect at each stop. No sightseeing today — just rest, good food, and a briefing.

2

Rift Valley Lakes — Abiata, Shala & Langano

Drive south from Addis into the East African Rift Valley. First stop: Lake Abiata-Shala National Park — thousands of lesser and greater flamingos turning the shallow soda lake pink, with white pelicans banking overhead. Afternoon at Lake Langano — the only bilharzia-free lake in Ethiopia, where you can actually swim. Sunset from the lodge veranda. Overnight at Langano.

3

Lake Hawassa — Hippos & Fish Market

Drive to Hawassa, the capital of the Southern Nations region. Morning at the famous Hawassa fish market — marabou storks, fish eagles, and pelicans congregate around the fishermen at the lakeshore in extraordinary numbers. Afternoon boat ride on Lake Hawassa to spot hippos and waterbirds. Evening in Hawassa town — vibrant, modern, very different from Addis.

4

Arba Minch & Nechisar National Park

Drive further south to Arba Minch — "forty springs" — perched between Lakes Abaya and Chamo. Morning boat trip in Nechisar National Park to the crocodile market (one of the densest Nile crocodile populations in Africa) and hippo pods along the shore. Afternoon free in Arba Minch to explore the Dorze weaving village above the town.

5

Omo Valley — Konso Cultural Landscape

Drive to the Konso Cultural Landscape (UNESCO) — a system of terraced hillside farms and dry-stone fortified villages built over generations by the Konso people. Walk through the villages with a local community guide (in addition to your main guide), visit the waga ancestor sculptures, and understand an agricultural system that has survived unchanged for 400 years. Overnight in Konso.

6

Bale Mountains — Sanetti Plateau

Drive northeast to the Bale Mountains National Park. Afternoon on the Sanetti Plateau at 4,000m — the largest area of Afro-alpine habitat in Africa. This is the best place in the world to see the Ethiopian wolf, Africa's most endangered carnivore. Also: mountain nyala, giant mole rat (endemic), and on a clear day, views across 200km of Ethiopian highlands. Overnight in Dinsho.

7

Return to Addis — Farewell

Drive back to Addis Ababa through the highlands. Final coffee ceremony and farewell lunch with your guide before airport transfer for your international departure. Seven days. Two UNESCO sites. An ecosystem found nowhere else on earth. You'll have a completely different picture of what Ethiopia is.
