Addis Deep Dive

"Three days to actually understand the city."

Home› Tours› Addis Deep Dive

Overview

Addis is a proper city. Treat it like one.

Most people pass through Addis on the way somewhere else. Three days is enough to stop passing through and start actually seeing it — the neighborhoods, the food scene, the contemporary art, the living history, the side of the capital that only residents and very well-guided visitors ever encounter.

This package combines the essential sites with deeper neighborhood walks, the contemporary art scene, a full food day (see also our standalone Food Journey tour), and a genuine local evening. It's also the opening two days of our Highlands & Heritage 12-day package — so it pairs perfectly if you're extending your trip.

Three Days

1History & Museums

National Museum (Lucy + Royal Regalia) → Ethnological Museum inside the former imperial palace → Holy Trinity Cathedral (Haile Selassie's tomb, Resistance memorial) → Red Terror Martyrs' Memorial Museum — the most important and most overlooked site in the city, documenting the Derg military junta's atrocities. Essential context for any diaspora visitor. Evening: orientation dinner at a traditional azmari bet (music house).

2Neighborhoods & Market

Piazza — the old Italian-era quarter with Art Deco buildings, street cafés, and the bookshop district → Mercato (Africa's largest open-air market, full guided walk through the textile, coffee, spice, and gold sections) → Shiro Meda (traditional handwoven fabric market) → neighborhood coffee ceremony in the afternoon. Evening: contemporary Addis — rooftop bar with city views, dinner at a local restaurant the tourists don't know.

3Art, Food & Farewell

Alle School of Fine Arts & Design — Ethiopia's leading contemporary art institution → gallery walk through the emerging Kazanchis art district → traditional food market visit with your guide (berbere, mitmita, teff) → full Ethiopian lunch tasting — injera, kitfo, tibs, gored gored, ayib, shiro — with your guide explaining the cultural significance of each dish. Airport transfer or hotel drop-off to close.

Best for: Second-time diaspora visitors who've done the churches and want to know the city. Also ideal as an opening to any multi-day tour — arrive a few days early and let Addis settle in.
