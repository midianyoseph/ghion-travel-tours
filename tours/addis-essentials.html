1 Day Addis Ababa Private Guide

Addis Essentials

"One full day. The Addis Ababa you came to see."

Home› Tours› Addis Essentials

Overview

One day. Done properly.

You have one day in Addis — maybe you're in transit, maybe you're adding a day before a longer trip, maybe you've just never seen the city properly before. This is the tour we built for exactly that situation: a full day with a private bilingual guide covering the places that actually matter.

Not a bus tour. Not a group. Just you, your guide, and a private vehicle — moving at your pace, stopping when something interests you, skipping what doesn't. This is also the most popular add-on for diaspora in transit through Bole who want more than just the airport.

The Day

8:30 AM
Hotel Pickup

Your guide meets you at your hotel with a private vehicle. Brief orientation over coffee.

9:00 AM
National Museum

Lucy — the 3.2-million-year-old Australopithecus afarensis skeleton. Also the Royal Regalia of Emperor Haile Selassie and the country's most significant archaeological collection.

10:30 AM
Ethnological Museum

Inside Haile Selassie's former bedroom palace on the Addis Ababa University campus. Traditional clothing, musical instruments, religious artifacts, and the bed where the Emperor slept.

12:00 PM
Lunch — Traditional Tej Bet

Lunch at a local tej bet (honey wine house) — kitfo, tibs, and injera. No tourist-facing restaurant. Your guide knows where the real food is.

2:00 PM
Holy Trinity Cathedral

Ethiopia's most important cathedral — the tomb of Emperor Haile Selassie and Empress Menen. The stained glass windows are extraordinary. Outside, the tombs of the Arbegnoch (Resistance fighters against the Italian occupation).

3:30 PM
Mercato

Africa's largest open-air market. Your guide navigates the textile, spice, coffee, and gold sections — and keeps you oriented in a market that covers several city blocks. This is not sightseeing. This is the city operating at full volume.

5:30 PM
Coffee Ceremony — Farewell

A traditional three-round coffee ceremony at a local home — roasted, ground, brewed in the jebena, served with popcorn. The genuine ritual, not a hotel performance. Drop-off at your hotel or Bole Airport on request.

Best for: Stopover travelers, diaspora with limited time, families wanting an orientation day before a longer trip, or anyone arriving early and wanting to use the day properly.
