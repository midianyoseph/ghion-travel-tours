Our Story

25 Years of Ethiopian Adventure

Ghion Travel and Tours (GTT) was established in 1998 in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with a singular vision: to share the extraordinary beauty, depth, and warmth of Ethiopia with the world. Named after the Ghion River — one of the four rivers flowing from the Garden of Eden according to ancient scriptures — our company has always embodied the spirit of discovery.

Over the past quarter-century, we have grown from a small, passionate team into one of Ethiopia's most respected and established tour operators, with an international reputation built entirely on the satisfaction of our guests.

We offer a complete range of travel services including expertly guided historical, cultural, and adventure tours; reliable car rentals; domestic flight ticketing; and hotel reservations across Ethiopia. Whether you are a first-time visitor or returning to deepen your connection with this ancient land, GTT is your trusted partner.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide our clients with the highest quality, most authentic, and professionally organized travel experiences in Ethiopia. We are committed to responsible tourism that creates lasting value for travelers, local communities, and the natural environment we love.

  • Deliver expertly curated itineraries tailored to each traveler
  • Support and empower local communities through tourism
  • Protect Ethiopia's unique natural and cultural heritage

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the leading Ethiopian tour operator — setting the standard for excellence, authenticity, and responsible travel. We envision a future where every visitor to Ethiopia becomes a lifelong ambassador for this extraordinary nation.

  • Champion Ethiopia's image as a world-class destination
  • Build bridges between Ethiopia and the global travel community
  • Continuously raise the bar for service quality and sustainability

The People Behind GTT

Meet Our Team

YG
Yoseph Getnet
General Manager & Founder

With over 25 years at the helm of Ghion Travel & Tours, Yoseph Getnet has built the company from the ground up with an unwavering commitment to excellence and a deep passion for sharing Ethiopia's treasures with the world. His extensive network, intimate knowledge of the country, and genuine warmth toward every client have made GTT the respected institution it is today.

Yoseph's expertise spans the full spectrum of Ethiopian travel — from the ancient churches of the north to the tribal cultures of the Omo Valley — and he remains personally involved in crafting exceptional experiences for every guest.
