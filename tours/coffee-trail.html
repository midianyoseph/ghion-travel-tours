The Coffee Trail

"From the source country. Follow it to your cup."

Home› Tours› The Coffee Trail

Overview

Coffee was born here. This is what that actually means.

Ethiopia is where coffee comes from. Not metaphorically — the wild coffee forests of Kaffa are the literal genetic origin of every cup of coffee ever drunk anywhere in the world. This half-day experience takes that story seriously.

We start at the source — the coffee market — and end with the full traditional ceremony in a local home. In between, you'll see how green beans are traded, how they're dry-roasted on a pan over charcoal, ground with a mortar and pestle, and brewed in the clay jebena. This is the complete coffee story, told in three hours in the city where it began.

The Trail

Stop 1
The Coffee Market — Merkato

Walk through Merkato's coffee section with your guide — sacks of green beans from Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Harrar, and Kaffa. Learn to identify varieties by smell and color. Watch the traders grade and negotiate. This is where the global supply chain starts.

Stop 2
Traditional Roastery

Visit a traditional coffee roaster — green beans dry-roasted on a flat iron pan over charcoal, stirred constantly by hand. The scent fills the whole street. You'll see the exact roast level decisions and the hand-testing that no machine has ever replicated.

Stop 3
A Specialty Coffee Bar

Addis has a thriving specialty coffee scene — Ethiopian baristas winning world championships. A short stop at one of the city's best cafés to taste single-origin Ethiopian coffees prepared by a trained barista. The contrast with the ceremony ahead is instructive.

Stop 4
The Full Ceremony — A Local Home

The traditional three-pour jebena ceremony in a local family home — not a hotel, not a restaurant. The hostess roasts, grinds, and brews fresh beans while burning incense on fresh grass. Three rounds of coffee: Abol (first, strongest), Tona (second, lighter), Baraka (third, a blessing). Served with popcorn and ambasha bread. This is how Ethiopia drinks coffee.

Best for: Anyone with a free morning or afternoon in Addis. Pairs perfectly with the Food Journey (afternoon). Can be added to the beginning or end of any tour as a standalone half-day.
