Day by Day

Full Itinerary

1–2

Addis Ababa — Deep Dive

Arrive at Bole International Airport. Two full days immersed in Addis with your bilingual guide: Mercato (Africa's largest open-air market), the National Museum (Lucy — 3.2M-year-old fossil), Holy Trinity Cathedral, the Ethnological Museum at Addis Ababa University inside the former imperial palace, a neighborhood coffee ceremony (the full three-pour jebena ritual), and Piazza — the old Italian quarter with a century of history in every building. Orientation dinner with your guide on Day 1 evening.

3

Gondar — Arrive, Fasil Ghebbi

Morning flight to Gondar. Check in, then straight to the Fasil Ghebbi Royal Enclosure (UNESCO) — six castles built by successive emperors between 1632 and 1855. In the late afternoon, visit Debre Berhan Selassie Church, its ceiling blanketed by 80 winged angel faces, one of the most photographed interiors in all of Africa.

4

Gondar — Fasilides Bath & Local Market

A slower day after the trek. Morning at leisure, then visit Fasilides Bath — the outdoor baptismal pool that fills each January for Timket, Ethiopia's most spectacular religious festival. Afternoon free to browse the local market or simply rest. Farewell dinner in Gondar before the flight tomorrow.

5

Bahir Dar — Lake Tana & Blue Nile Falls

Fly from Gondar to Bahir Dar, a beautiful lakeside city on the southern shore of Lake Tana — the largest lake in Ethiopia and the source of the Blue Nile. Morning: boat trip to visit the island monasteries of Lake Tana, some dating to the 13th and 14th centuries, containing extraordinary ancient manuscripts, crowns, and religious paintings rarely seen by outsiders. Afternoon: drive to the Blue Nile Falls (Tis Abay) — one of Africa's great waterfalls, where the Blue Nile begins its journey north to Sudan and Egypt. Sunset views across the lake from Bahir Dar. Overnight in the city.

6–7

Lalibela — The Rock-Hewn Churches

Fly to Lalibela. Two full days with all 11 rock-hewn churches, carved directly from volcanic rock in the 12th century — a UNESCO World Heritage Site that still functions as a living pilgrimage destination. Day 6: the northern cluster and Bete Medhane Alem — the largest rock-hewn church in the world. Day 7: Bete Giyorgis at sunrise, a perfect cruciform church descending 12 metres below ground, surrounded by mist and the sound of chanting. Evenings: local dinners with your guide, time to absorb what you've seen.

8

Lalibela Surrounds — Remote Monasteries

A day beyond the main churches. Mule ride or short trek to Asheten Mariam Monastery — perched high above Lalibela with views across the highlands for 100km in every direction. In the afternoon, drive to Yimrehane Krestos — a pre-Lalibela cave church with finely decorated Aksumite architecture, rarely visited and genuinely stunning. Fewer than 10% of visitors to Lalibela make it here.

9

Return to Addis — Farewell

Fly directly from Lalibela back to Addis Ababa. Final coffee ceremony and farewell lunch with your guide — a proper debrief on the 10 days, what each place meant, and where to go next time. Airport transfer for international departure. You will leave knowing you did not scratch the surface — you went all the way in.
