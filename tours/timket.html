Timket in Lalibela

"One of the greatest living religious spectacles on earth."

Home›Tours›Timket in Lalibela

Overview

Witness 1,700 years of living faith.

Timket — the Ethiopian Orthodox celebration of Christ's baptism — is celebrated every January 19–20, and nowhere on earth is it more extraordinary than Lalibela. Tens of thousands of white-robed pilgrims descend on the ancient rock-hewn churches. The tabot (a replica of the Ark of the Covenant) is carried in procession by priests in ceremonial robes, accompanied by singing, ululation, and the sound of the sistrum and kebero drum through the night.

This is not a tourist performance. This is Ethiopia's most sacred annual ceremony — still practiced exactly as it was for over a thousand years. Our guide is deeply versed in Ethiopian Orthodox theology and will give you the context to understand what you are witnessing. We coordinate your ceremonial access and position carefully.

Day by Day

Full Itinerary

1

Fly to Lalibela — Church Orientation

Morning flight from Addis to Lalibela. Afternoon orientation walk through the town — getting your bearings and understanding the layout of the 11 churches before the ceremony begins. Full guided tour of the churches with deep historical and liturgical context: the significance of each church, the saints they honor, the architectural symbolism, and what to expect over the next two days. Evening dinner — your guide explains the full Timket sequence so you arrive at each moment prepared.

2

Timket Eve — The Procession of the Tabot

This is the evening everything builds toward. As the sun sets, priests emerge from each of the 11 churches carrying the tabot (the altar tablet, treated as a replica of the Ark of the Covenant) on their heads, wrapped in embroidered cloth and shaded by ceremonial umbrellas. The procession moves through the town to the baptismal pool, accompanied by drums, sistrums, prayer chants, and the ululation of thousands of pilgrims. Your guide explains every element in real time. Optional: all-night vigil at the pool with the pilgrims — music, prayer, and community through the night.

3

Timket Day — Holy Water & Celebration

The holy water ceremony: priests bless the pool and the faithful are sprinkled with water (or immerse themselves), renewing their baptism vows. The tabots return to their churches in a second procession. The rest of the day belongs to community celebration — music, traditional food, dancing, and the extraordinary sight of the entire town in white. Rest in the afternoon. Evening: dinner and quiet reflection with your guide.

4

Return to Addis

Morning at leisure in Lalibela — final walk, last coffee, last look. Optional half-day in Addis Ababa if time permits. Airport transfer for your international departure.
