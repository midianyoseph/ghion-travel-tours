Arba Minch

"Crocodiles, crater lakes, and highland tribes — Ethiopia's south in three days."

Home› Tours› Arba Minch

Overview

The south in three unforgettable days.

Arba Minch sits at the edge of the Rift Valley where two lakes — Abaya and Chamo — meet the highlands. In three days you'll cross them both: a boat excursion on Lake Chamo to witness the famous crocodile market, a visit to the natural springs, and a drive up to Chencha Mountain to meet the Dorze people, one of Ethiopia's most skilled highland communities.

Short enough to add on to another trip, rich enough to stand on its own. Your guide handles all transport, boat hire, and village access — you just show up and take it in.

Day by Day

Full Itinerary

1

Arba Minch — Arrival, Forty Springs & Lake Chamo

Morning flight from Addis Ababa to Arba Minch. Your guide meets you at the airport and takes you first to the Forty Springs — the natural spring complex from which the city takes its name, flowing through thick forest into the Rift Valley lakes below. Afternoon: board a local boat on Lake Chamo for the famous crocodile market — a sandbank on the lake where enormous Nile crocodiles gather in numbers rarely seen anywhere else in Africa. The boat excursion also takes you past hippo pods and fish eagles along the shoreline. Dinner and overnight in Arba Minch.

2

Chencha Mountain — The Dorze People

Drive up into the Gamo highlands to Chencha Mountain, rising to over 3,000 metres above the Rift Valley floor. Here you'll visit a Dorze community — a highland people known across Ethiopia for their extraordinary skill in hand-weaving and for their towering bamboo huts, some standing up to 12 metres tall and shaped like elephant heads. Walk through the village with your guide, see the weaving process up close, and share a local meal of enset bread (false banana — the Dorze staple). The views back down to the twin lakes from the ridge are some of the finest in southern Ethiopia. Return to Arba Minch for dinner and overnight.

3

Return to Addis Ababa

Morning at leisure in Arba Minch — time for a final walk along the lake or a last coffee before your guide transfers you to the airport. Flight back to Addis Ababa. The package ends on arrival at Bole International Airport.
