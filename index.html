Why Travel With Us

We built this for you specifically.

Most tour operators weren't thinking about diaspora when they designed their packages. We were. Here's what that means in practice.

25 Years. The Same Family.

Ghion Travel was founded in 1998 by Yoseph Getnet. The same family has run every trip since. When you call, you're talking to the owner — not a call center.

Your Guide Speaks Amharic.

Every diaspora trip uses a bilingual English/Amharic guide. They understand the cultural weight of the trip — and they won't rush you past a moment that matters.

24/7 US-Based Emergency Contact

Every multi-destination package includes a US-based emergency contact number. Your family in America can reach a person — not a voicemail — if something comes up.

We Handle Everything In-Country

Domestic flights, hotels, private vehicle, all entrance fees, airport transfers. You land in Addis and your guide is there. You leave knowing we handled every logistics detail.

Families, Groups, Solo — All Welcome

Three-generation family trips. Groups of friends from the diaspora. Solo travelers who want company. Our packages scale with group size and our guides adapt to every dynamic.

Internationally Recognized

Proud members of SafariBookings, the National Tour Association (NTA), Skål International, and the African Travel & Tourism Association (ATTA).
